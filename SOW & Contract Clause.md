# projectai LLP — Standard SOW & Contract Clauses

> **Document Purpose:**  
> This document contains legally protective standard clauses and disclaimers to insert into your **Client Quotations**, **Proposals**, **Statements of Work (SOW)**, and **Master Services Agreements (MSA)** for enterprise AI automation and project management engagements.

---

## 1. ROI & Performance Projections Disclaimer (Essential)

*Insert this clause into all Proposals, Quotations, and SOW agreements where ROI calculations or time-saving figures are referenced.*

```markdown
### Clause: Projections & Performance Disclaimer
Any return on investment (ROI), operational hours saved, efficiency percentages, or financial projections provided by projectai LLP (whether in preliminary scoping discussions, marketing materials, web calculators, or this Statement of Work) are estimates provided strictly for illustrative and strategic planning purposes. 

Actual operational velocity, time savings, and business outcomes depend on Client's internal team adoption, adherence to established operating procedures, data quality, and third-party service stability. Such projections shall not be construed as a performance guarantee, SLA, or warranty of specific economic outcomes.
```

## 2. Third-Party AI Models & API Dependencies

*Protects against upstream changes from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Make, n8n, etc.*

```markdown
### Clause: Third-Party Services & Model Dependencies
The solutions delivered by projectai LLP may interact with third-party foundation model APIs, cloud providers, and SaaS platforms (e.g., Anthropic, OpenAI, Google Cloud, n8n, CRM/ERP vendors). 

projectai LLP shall not be held liable for:
1. Service disruptions, outages, or latency caused by third-party API providers.
2. Changes in third-party model pricing, rate limits, deprecations, or policy modifications.
3. Natural probabilistic variances or non-deterministic outputs inherent to Large Language Model (LLM) architectures.
```

## 3. Data Privacy, Confidentiality & Model Training Guarantee

*Reassures enterprise clients regarding Singapore PDPA and corporate IP protection.*

```markdown
### Clause: Data Privacy & Non-Training Assurance
projectai LLP covenants that:
1. Client data processed through configured enterprise zero-data-retention (ZDR) endpoints shall not be utilized to train, fine-tune, or improve public foundation models.
2. All proprietary business logic, vector embeddings, and database records remain the exclusive confidential property of the Client.
3. All implementation architectures comply with the Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) of Singapore and prevailing industry encryption standards (TLS 1.3 in transit, AES-256 at rest).
```

## 4. Post-Deployment Support vs. Ongoing Maintenance

*Clarifies the boundary between project delivery and recurring maintenance.*

```markdown
### Clause: Handover, Hypercare & Maintenance Boundary
1. **Hypercare Period:** Following final user acceptance testing (UAT) and production deployment, projectai LLP provides a complimentary [e.g., 14-Day / 30-Day] hypercare support window for system stabilization, prompt tuning, and bug remediation within the agreed original scope.
2. **Scope Boundary:** Requests for new workflow automations, additional third-party tool integrations, or modifications necessitated by upstream API schema changes post-hypercare shall require a separate Change Order or an active Monthly Maintenance Care Agreement.
```

## 5. Client Obligations & System Access

*Ensures clients provide timely access and prevents project stalls.*

```markdown
### Clause: Client Responsibilities & Data Readiness
Timely delivery of the project relies on the Client providing:
1. Prompt administrative or sandbox access to relevant APIs, database schemas, and tool credentials.
2. Clean, representative sample data for pipeline testing and prompt evaluation.
3. Designated stakeholder availability for scheduled milestone reviews and UAT sign-offs within [3 business days] of delivery.
```

## 6. Limitation of Liability

*Caps financial exposure to project fees paid.*

```markdown
### Clause: Limitation of Liability
To the maximum extent permitted by applicable Singapore law, projectai LLP's total aggregate liability arising out of or related to this Agreement (whether in contract, tort, or otherwise) shall not exceed the total fees actually paid by Client to projectai LLP under this specific Statement of Work in the preceding three (3) months. In no event shall projectai LLP be liable for any indirect, incidental, consequential, or special damages, including lost profits or business interruption.
```
